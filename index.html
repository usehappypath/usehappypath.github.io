How it works

  1. Install the app on the repo of your website. It gets one right: issues. It cannot read your code.
  2. Tell it your site. It opens a "Set up Happy Path" issue. Reply /happypath setup https://your-site.com and put a small file on your site to prove it is yours.
  3. It tests every day. Pages, links, errors, phone layout and speed, read-only. Once a week it also reads your site like a real visitor: does the page keep its promise, is the content clear, what would confuse people.
  4. Only confirmed bugs become issues. Each one is reproduced twice in a fresh browser first, with the page, the steps, what happens and what a user expects. At most 10 new issues a day.
  5. Answer in one line. It rechecks the live site and closes or reopens the issue with proof.

The one-line answers

/happypath fixedAfter the fix is live. It tests again, then closes or reopens with proof.
/happypath cant-reproIt replays twice. Gone: closed. Still there: proof posted.
/happypath wontfix <why>Intended, or not worth it. A reason is required.
/happypath duplicate #12Merged into the other issue.
/happypath infoPage, steps and the last check.

Closing an issue yourself works too: "completed" is rechecked like fixed.

Questions

Does it change anything on my site?

No. It only reads and clicks safe things like links, tabs and menus. It never submits forms, signs up, signs in, buys or sends messages.

Can it read my code?

No. The GitHub app has one right: read and write issues in the repos you choose.

Why do I need to put a file on my site?

So nobody can point Happy Path at a site they do not own. Serve /.well-known/happypath.txt containing your repo name, like owner/repo.

How many issues will I get?

Only bugs reproduced twice, at medium severity or above by default, and at most 10 new issues a day. One issue per problem, never duplicates.

What does it cost?

Nothing during early access. We test a limited number of sites at a time; when full, new sites join a waitlist and the setup issue tells you your place.

Does it test pages behind a login?

Not yet for new sites. Public pages only.

How do I stop it?

Uninstall the app in your GitHub settings. Testing stops at once, and we delete your site's data within 30 days.
