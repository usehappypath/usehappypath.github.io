What we collect

  • From GitHub: the account and repos where you install the app, and the issues and comments Happy Path takes part in. The app cannot read your code.
  • From your website: what any visitor can see on public pages: page text, screenshots, links, load times and browser errors.

What we do not collect

  • We do not sign in, submit forms, or read pages behind a login on your site.
  • We do not collect data about your site's visitors.
  • We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

How we use it

Only to test your site, file and update issues in your repo, and recheck fixes. Page text may be read by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to judge clarity and content quality. Screenshots and page data stay on our servers.

How long we keep it

While the app is installed. After you uninstall it, we delete your site's data within 30 days. Issues already in your repo stay there; they are yours.

Your choices

Uninstall the app at any time in your GitHub settings. To ask what we hold or to have it deleted sooner, write to CONTACT_EMAIL.
